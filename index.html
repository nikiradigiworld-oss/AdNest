Open Anaar App → Settings → Profile

Tap Advertiser under your profile options. This opens a separate advertiser window.

2

Enter Phone Number

Only your mobile number is needed. You'll receive a one-time password (OTP) to verify.

3

Complete OTP Verification

Enter the OTP from your SMS. Verification is instant and secure.

4
